Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your email address and a securely hashed password. As you use the Service, we store the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type and general usage patterns, to help us maintain and improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide the core functionality of Spendly: letting you log expenses, view spending summaries, and manage your account. Your data may also be used to communicate important account or service updates, and to diagnose and fix technical issues. We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. While we take security seriously, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be removed from our active systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for functionality such as font delivery or hosting infrastructure. These providers only process the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the support channels listed in the app.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
